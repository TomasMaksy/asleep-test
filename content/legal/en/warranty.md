We want you to sleep well on asleep for years. Manufacturing is a human process, and a defect can occasionally occur. If that happens, we will do everything we reasonably can to fix it.

You can expect our products to meet the stated specifications, the reasonable requirements of normal use, and the legal provisions applicable at the time of order. We therefore provide a warranty on (certain parts of) our products.

## 1. The warranty expires if

- The product was used incorrectly or not according to the care instructions
- The product was modified, repaired, or cleaned by a third party without our approval
- Damage results from normal wear and tear, misuse, or external causes

## 2. Mattress warranty — 10 years

asleep mattresses come with a **10-year warranty** covering manufacturing defects under normal household use. Full coverage details and exclusions will be listed here.

## 3. Covers and accessories

Warranty periods for covers and accessories (if sold) will be listed here, including what is and is not covered.

## 4. How to use your warranty

Contact us via the [contact page](/contact) with your order details and a description of the issue. We may ask for photos to assess the claim.

## 5. Warranty process

If a covered defect is confirmed, we will repair or replace the product, or offer another suitable remedy, according to the warranty terms and applicable law.

## 6. Care and maintenance

Follow the care instructions supplied with your product. Proper care helps preserve comfort and keeps the warranty valid.
