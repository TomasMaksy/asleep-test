## Management

asleep is the controller of the personal data described on this page. For privacy questions, contact us via the [contact page](/contact).

## Visitor data

When you browse asleep.lt we may process technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited. This helps us keep the site secure and understand how it is used.

## Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies and similar technologies for essential site functions, analytics, and marketing measurement. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## Data you provide

When you place an order, contact support, or subscribe to updates, we process the details you submit — for example name, email, phone number, and delivery address — to fulfil your request.

## Sharing with third parties

We share personal data with service providers only when needed to run the business (payments, delivery, hosting, analytics). We do not sell your personal data.

## Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data. Contact us and we will help where we can.

## Questions

If anything on this page is unclear, [get in touch](/contact). We usually reply within one business day.
